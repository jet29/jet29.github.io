Small procedural things

Randomness, a rule, and time. Nothing to do but watch.

  1. Galton boardBalls flip a coin at every peg and pile up into a bell curve. The slider tilts the board.
  2. Random walkA few thousand particles step at random. The cloud spreads exactly as fast as the maths says it should.
  3. Diffusion-limited aggregationParticles wander until they touch the crystal and stick. A coral grows out of noise.
  4. Noise planetA world from layered noise: oceans, coasts, mountains, snow. A new one every click.
  5. Strange attractorsThirty thousand particles following Lorenz and friends. Deterministic, never repeating.
